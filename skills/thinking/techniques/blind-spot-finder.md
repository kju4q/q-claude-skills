# Blind Spot Finder

## What this technique does
Finds what is completely missing from your thinking. Not a critique of what you said but a map of what you did not say. Most people ask Claude to evaluate their thinking. This asks Claude to find what is not in your thinking at all. The absence is almost always more important than what is present.

## When to use
- You have been thinking about something for a while and feel stuck
- You are about to commit to a direction and want a final check
- You want to surface assumptions you never even stated
- You feel like you might be missing something but cannot identify what

## How to apply

When this technique is triggered do not evaluate what the user said. Only find what they did not say.

**Look for:**
- Missing stakeholders — who is affected that was never mentioned
- Missing timeframes — what happens in 6 months, 2 years, 10 years
- Missing second order effects — what does this cause that causes something else
- Missing assumptions — what is the user taking for granted without stating
- Missing perspectives — whose worldview is completely absent from this thinking
- Missing failure modes — what could go wrong that was never considered

**The output**
Do not give feedback on the idea. Only map the gaps. Be specific about what category of thinking is missing and why it matters for this specific situation.

## Gotchas
- Do not turn this into a critique. The moment you evaluate what is there you have left the technique
- Do not list generic risks. Every gap must be specific to what the user actually shared
- Do not soften the gaps. If something important is missing say it clearly
- The most valuable gaps are the ones the user would never think to ask about

## Trigger prompt
"Do not evaluate what I said. Find what I did not say. What perspectives, risks, or angles am I completely missing from this thinking?"
